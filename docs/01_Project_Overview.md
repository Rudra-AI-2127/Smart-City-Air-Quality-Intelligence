# Smart City – Air Quality and Pollution Intelligence

## Project Title
Smart City – Air Quality and Pollution Intelligence: An End-to-End Data Engineering Pipeline Using Databricks, PySpark, Delta Lake and Power BI

## Real-World Problem Statement

Air pollution is a major environmental and public health concern in Indian cities. Air quality data is often distributed across multiple cities, monitoring stations and time intervals, making it difficult to analyze pollution trends, compare locations and understand the factors associated with poor air quality.

The objective of this project is to develop a scalable data engineering pipeline that integrates historical air quality and weather datasets into a unified analytical platform.

The project uses Python, Apache Spark, Databricks and Delta Lake to ingest, clean, transform and organize data through Bronze, Silver and Gold layers. The processed datasets support analysis of city-wise AQI trends, pollutant concentrations, hourly pollution patterns, monitoring-station hotspots and relationships between weather conditions and air quality.

## Project Objectives

1. Ingest historical air pollution datasets into cloud-based storage.
2. Integrate historical weather data using an API.
3. Implement Bronze, Silver and Gold layers using Databricks and Delta Lake.
4. Clean and validate pollutant, location and date information.
5. Generate analytics-ready datasets for pollution intelligence.
6. Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate key insights.

## Expected Outcome

A reproducible data engineering solution that transforms raw environmental data into structured analytical datasets, enabling comparisons across cities, stations and time periods and supporting data-informed environmental monitoring.
